Twisted Gossip – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Twisted Gossip ("the game") is a puzzle game made by Little Mischief Studio. This page explains what information the game uses.

What the game stores on your phone

Your level progress, stars, sound setting, hint timing and whether you bought "Remove ads" are saved only on your device. We do not have accounts, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address) and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice anytime with the "Privacy choices" button on the home screen.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Purchases (Google Play)

If you buy "Remove ads", the payment is handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google Play tells the game only that the purchase was made.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
